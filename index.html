Knee Decompression Therapy in Lighthouse Point

Knee pain can limit your ability to walk, exercise, or simply enjoy daily life. Whether you're dealing with meniscus irritation, cartilage wear, or lingering discomfort after an injury, you have options beyond resting or considering surgery. At McNamara Chiropractic Center in Lighthouse Point, knee decompression using Knee-on-Trac® therapy offers a non-surgical approach to reducing joint stress and supporting healing.

How Knee-on-Trac® Works

Knee-on-Trac® is a specialized decompression device designed to gently separate the bones of the knee joint, reducing pressure on cartilage and soft tissues. By creating space within the joint, the therapy may help decrease inflammation, improve fluid circulation, and allow injured structures to begin repairing themselves. The process is gentle and non-invasive—there's no manipulation, forcing, or downtime involved.

This approach makes sense for many patients: if your knee joint is being compressed and irritated, relieving that pressure can be genuinely helpful. Dr. Carol McNamara-Krauss and her team assess your individual situation honestly and will tell you whether knee decompression may be right for your specific condition.

What Knee Decompression May Help

Patients often turn to Knee-on-Trac® therapy for conditions like patellar tendinitis, degenerative joint changes, and post-injury pain where conservative care hasn't brought relief. The therapy works alongside other treatments—adjustments, therapeutic massage, and custom orthotics—to address the full picture of your knee health.

Common Questions

Does insurance cover knee decompression? Coverage varies. Most major health plans cover medically necessary chiropractic care, including some therapeutic modalities, though limits and copays apply. For auto-accident injuries in Florida, Personal Injury Protection (PIP) covers initial care if treatment begins within 14 days of the accident. Contact the office to discuss your specific policy.

Is knee decompression safe? Knee-on-Trac® therapy is designed to be gentle and low-risk for most patients. Your health history will be reviewed thoroughly so the team can identify any conditions that might make the therapy unsuitable for you.

How long does treatment take? Treatment plans are customized based on your condition's severity and your body's response. Some patients notice improvement within a few weeks, while others require a longer course of therapy.
